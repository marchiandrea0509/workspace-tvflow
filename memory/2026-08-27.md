# 2026-08-27

## Bitget MAE/MFE eight-day audit

- Andrea asked whether high planned R:R could be evaluated with candle data for seven/eight days after each trade entry. Implemented `scripts/evaluate_bitget_trade_excursions.py` and ran the final audit with eight-day horizons and Bitget five-minute USDT-futures candles.
- Reconstructed 62 completed position lifecycles across 26 symbols from `raw_bitget_history_latest.json`. The method snapshots FIFO lots at maximum filled exposure, reports live-trade excursion only until the actual close, and separately reports an eight-day post-entry counterfactual. Saved candles begin at the literal first fill and extend through eight days after maximum exposure.
- Discovered Bitget fills-export fragmentation: small fill fragments can be absent even though orders-history contains the exact filled `baseVolume`. Hardened reconstruction to reconcile each order once at its last observed fill using orders-history volume and to use order-level `totalProfits`; final validation had zero orphan close quantity and zero phantom active lifecycle, matching the empty live position snapshot.
- Final sample result: R:R below 2.5 had 31 trades, about `+1579 USDT` gross, 23/31 reached +1R while open, and 26/31 reached planned reward within eight days. R:R at/above 2.5 had 31 trades, 25 stops, about `-1108 USDT` gross, only 12/31 reached +1R while open, 3/31 reached full planned reward while open, and 10/31 reached planned reward within eight days.
- High-R:R attribution is mixed but concrete: median risk distance `1.70%`, reward distance `6.59%`, and first-fill-to-maximum-exposure span `17h` versus about `6m` below 2.5R. Stretch TP dependency is primary; narrower stops and deep-ladder adverse selection are secondary. Six stopped high-R:R trades had offered at least +1R, six stopped then reached target later within eight days, and nine never reached +1R even in the counterfactual.
